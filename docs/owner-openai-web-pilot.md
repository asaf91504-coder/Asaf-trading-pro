# OpenAI web research pilot (off by default)

Only the explicitly configured owner, who must also be an administrator with a live authenticated session, can opt into the pilot from the chat's web-search control. This sends the final current question and fixed research instructions to OpenAI. It never transfers attached charts, ticker selection, images, previous turns, customer identifiers or provider data. Search does not authorize real-time or standalone quote redistribution. No model-supplied chart actions are returned.

Activation requires explicit approval in each deployment context and a deliberately selected total of 1–10 attempts: `ASAF_OWNER_OPENAI_WEB_PILOT_APPROVED=true` and `ASAF_OWNER_OPENAI_WEB_PILOT_TOTAL=<1–10>`. The app reserves each attempt atomically before calling the provider, without application-credit debits, refunds, rotation or automatic retries. The configured owner burst limit also applies. Clearing or replacing storage/account infrastructure would invalidate existing quota state; do not do so during the pilot. Do not enable in previews unless that separate allowance is intended.

A request permits at most one hosted web tool call and 700 output tokens, with low search context. Consult the official pricing and your organization's actual billing/credits before activation. A cap on attempts is not an exact dollar cap because inference and search-content tokens are independently billable. Current tool-call fees for up to ten attempts: up to $0.10 at $0.01 per call, plus applicable token charges, not a contractual maximum. The existing model request ceiling does not cover this new tool. Do not activate if the owner requires a hard dollar cap you cannot enforce. No subscription or API budget has been changed by adding this code.

A launch check should verify official endpoint and account tool access, citation parsing/placement against one real owner result, locale compliance and source recency, then verify a small approved suite of questions on U.S. market, public company filings and crypto. Do not assert general browsing, customer access, complete market coverage, or licensed quote provision from this pilot.
